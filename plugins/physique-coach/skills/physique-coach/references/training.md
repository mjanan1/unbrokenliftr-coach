# Training

The method: low volume, high effort. Few exercises, few sets, every working set taken close to or to failure, each muscle trained at least twice a week, with progressive overload every session.

## Pick the split by days available
Every muscle group gets trained at least twice a week.
- **4 days: Upper / Lower, twice a week** (default). For example Mon Upper, Tue Lower, Wed rest, Thu Upper, Fri Lower, weekend off or easy cardio.
- **6 days: Push / Pull / Legs, twice**, with a rest day: Push, Pull, Legs, rest, Push, Pull, Legs.
- **2-3 days: Full body**, at least twice a week.
Days can move around work. Put a rest day between hard sessions when possible.

## Exercises per session
| Muscle group | Exercises | Sets |
|---|---|---|
| Chest | 2 different exercises (e.g. a press and an incline or fly) | 2-3 each (confirm) |
| Back | 2 different exercises, including pull-ups or a vertical pull | 2-3 each (confirm) |
| Legs | 3-4 exercises, including calves | 2-3 each (confirm) |
| Biceps | 1 exercise | 2 max |
| Triceps | 1 exercise | 2 max |
| Side delts | Lateral raises | 2 max |
| Front delts | No direct work: covered by chest pressing | — |
| Rear delts | No direct work: covered by pull-ups and rows | — |

## Reps and effort
- 5-8 reps per set. High-quality reps with full control; a rep that breaks form doesn't count.
- Every working set close to failure; some sets to failure.
- When recovering from injury or surgery, or after a layoff: stop 2-3 reps short of failure until cleared, and the surgeon's or doctor's limits override this plan.

## Rest between sets
Not timed. Rest as long as you need to be fully recovered so the next set can be pushed all the way. For heavy compound lifts that is often 2-4 minutes.

## Progressive overload
Log every session: exercise, sets, reps, load.
- When every working set reaches 8 clean reps, add the smallest weight step next session and work back up from 5.
- If reps or load haven't gone up on a lift in 3 weeks, check sleep, food and effort first, then swap to a close variation of the exercise.
(Progression rule is a default: owner to confirm.)

## Effort check (because the volume is low)
With only 2 sets per small muscle, the sets have to be hard. If the user says sets "felt easy", reps aren't going up, or they finish sessions fresh, the problem is effort before it is programme. Ask how many more reps they could have done on their last set; the answer should be 0-2.

## Cardio
Easy Zone 2 (full sentences possible; with a heart-rate strap, roughly 60-70% of max) once or twice a week is fine. Keep hard or long cardio away from the day before legs.

## Stalls (order of checks)
1. Is the log honest (food and training)?
2. Are sets actually close to failure?
3. Sleep under 7 hours?
4. Protein floor hit at least 6 days of 7?
5. Has load or reps gone up in the last 3 weeks? If not, change the exercise variation.
6. Only then change calories, using the adjustment rules in SKILL.md.
