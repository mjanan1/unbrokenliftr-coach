# Training

> **OWNER TO REPLACE.** This file holds a conservative default so the skill works. The coach's own programme, progression rules and exercise choices go here.

## Default week (4 lifting days)
- Mon Upper, Tue Lower, Wed rest or easy cardio, Thu Upper, Fri Lower, Sat easy Zone 2 run or swim, Sun off.
- Beginners (under about 6 months of consistent lifting): 3 full-body sessions a week instead.
- Days can move around work; keep a hard run or long session away from the day before lower body.

## Progression (default)
- Main lifts in the 5-10 rep range, accessories 8-15.
- 10-20 hard sets per muscle group per week, each set ending 1-3 reps short of failure.
- Double progression: when every set hits the top of the rep range with good form, add the smallest weight step next session.
- Log every session: exercise, sets, reps, load. No log, no judgment on progress.

## Cardio
- Zone 2: a pace where nasal breathing or full sentences are possible. With a heart-rate strap, roughly 60-70% of max heart rate.
- One or two easy sessions a week have little effect on muscle gain. Large volumes of hard running do.

## Stalls (default order of checks)
1. Is the log honest (food and training)?
2. Sleep under 7 hours?
3. Protein floor hit at least 6 days of 7?
4. Has load or reps gone up in the last 3 weeks? If not, change the rep range or exercise variation.
5. Only then change calories, using the adjustment rules in SKILL.md.
